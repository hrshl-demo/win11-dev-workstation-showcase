    A developer coding demo with exact source prompts and safe fallback. The 15 minutes excludes laptop provisioning, approvals, package/image restoration and cloud delivery.

    APurpose and place in the demo

    Show a small financial behavior change, truthful checks, a human-run prepared sample and a protected existing-AKS design.

    Do not claim timed laptop setup, recorded output as live proof, completed modernization or cloud deployment. Stop when prerequisites fail.

    BStarting state

    • Approved setup/profile/source acceptance, explicit Dependencies Restore, daily checks and rehearsal completed separately by the human.

    • Required GitHub/Copilot access and optional Java 11/modernization prerequisites arranged beforehand; no live installation/repair.
